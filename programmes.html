Choosing

Which route actually fits.

The most common mistake is buying more support than the situation needs, and the second most common is buying less. Both waste money.

Workflow pack

Fits if: you can define your target output and its standard without help, you have time to work through it, and your main need is structure rather than judgment.

Does not fit if: you are not sure what "good" looks like for your output. Stage 1 will stall and everything after it inherits the problem.

Live workshop

Fits if: you have real work to build against and want the design decisions challenged as you make them. Also the right answer when a team needs a shared standard.

Does not fit if: you have no live project. The method tests against real work; a hypothetical produces a hypothetical workflow.

Coaching and review

Fits if: you have a workflow that mostly works and specific decisions or failures you cannot resolve alone.

Does not fit if: you want ongoing unlimited support. It is deliberately bounded — see what that means.
