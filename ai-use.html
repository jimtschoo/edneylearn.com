Disclosure

How we use AI in our own work.

We sell workflow transparency and evidence discipline. It would be difficult to do that credibly while being vague about our own practice, so here it is.

Where AI is used

Edney Learn uses AI inside its own designed workflows for content drafting and structuring, research synthesis, product and course architecture, internal operating procedures, and the construction of reference workflows. This is the "internal operations" and "product generation" application context described in the method.

Every one of those workflows was designed using the same ten-stage method we teach, with the same allocation decisions, verification steps and escalation triggers. We are not exempt from our own method.

What a human owns here

  • Every claim made on this site, and whether the evidence behind it supports it.
  • Every status label attached to a product.
  • Methodology decisions, product architecture and quality standards.
  • Assessment judgments on any future microcredential or evidence portfolio.
  • Any statement about a customer, their work, or their outcome.
  • Final acceptance of anything published.

None of those are delegated to a model, and the workflows are designed so they cannot be by accident.

Where our own workflows stop

The same escalation triggers we build for customers apply to us. Claims that would outrun their evidence get stopped and sent back. Anything touching customer confidentiality or intellectual property escalates. Legal, financial and regulated matters go to qualified professionals. Where source material is insufficient, the workflow requests it rather than generating something plausible.

Content produced with AI assistance

Written material on this site and in our products is produced through AI-augmented workflows with human authorship, judgment, verification and final acceptance. We do not publish unreviewed generated text, and we do not present generated material as the product of research or experience that did not happen.

Attribution in derived content

Where our content draws on another author's published work — particularly in the Book Impact channel — the source is attributed and the material is genuinely generalised into transferable best practice. We do not repackage a named author's proprietary framework and present it as our own.

Why this page exists

Because the first question a sceptical professional should ask a company selling AI capability is what that company's own practice looks like. If the answer is unavailable, the sceptic has learned something.

Found a problem?

If something on this site reads as though a claim has outrun its evidence, or as though a human judgment was made by a machine, that is a workflow failure on our side. Tell us — we would rather fix it than defend it.

What we use, and how your data is handled.

Tools in use

Edney Learn uses large language models in its internal content development, method documentation and operational workflows.

Customer data

Edney Learn uses AI on its own operational content. Where client engagement material is involved, the handling of that material, including whether any AI processing occurs, is agreed as part of the engagement terms, not assumed. Your work is not used to train AI models.

Regulatory position

No sector-specific AI disclosure regulation currently applies to Edney Learn's services. This notice is updated as obligations emerge.
