Common core

Seven dimensions that apply to every profession.

These are the constructs that separate someone who uses AI from someone whose professional workflow is genuinely AI-augmented. They do not change by role.

  • 01
    Task-selection judgmentCan I tell which parts of my work AI should touch, which it shouldn't, and say why?
  • 02
    Human–AI allocationDo I divide the work deliberately between my judgment, the model and other tools, or by default?
  • 03
    Verification practiceWhat do I actually check, how, and does the check happen before the output leaves me?
  • 04
    Workflow integrationDoes my AI use sit inside a defined sequence with inputs, outputs and quality gates, or beside it as a habit?
  • 05
    AccountabilityIs it explicit who owns the decision, the claim and the consequence?
  • 06
    Evidence practiceDoes operating my workflow leave a record a client, employer or assessor could examine?
  • 07
    Transfer into workDoes the capability show up in real professional output, or only in exercises?

What this profile is not

It is not a certification, a licence, or a statement of verified professional competence, and self-reported confidence is not competence. It is a developmental self-assessment. Assessed, verified capability would come later, through authentic-performance assessment against a future microcredential standard that is not yet available.
