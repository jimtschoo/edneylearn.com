Audiences

Professionals and organisations who want capability, not activity.

The distinction that matters is not your job title. It is whether you are trying to build something you can keep, measure and demonstrate, or trying to get more done this week. We are good at the first and openly not the point for the second.

Individual professionals

You produce knowledge work to a professional standard and you are accountable for it. You have used AI enough to see both what it does well and where it quietly fails. You want a system, not more tips.

First step: take the Benchmark, or go straight to the Author or Consultant pathway.

The individual route

Managers and teams

Your team has tool access and inconsistent practice. You need a shared standard, visible quality control and some way of knowing what is actually happening across the group.

First step available now: organisational benchmarking by arrangement.

The team route

SMEs

No dedicated learning function, no capacity for a twelve-month transformation programme, and a real need for AI use that is consistent and defensible rather than improvised.

First step: register for the Benchmark and start with one workflow.

The SME route

Associations and universities

You deliver professional development to members or students and need AI capability content that is methodologically sound, assessable and defensible to your own quality processes.

First step available now: a scoped pilot with your own cohort.

Honest fit

Who this is not for.

Naming this saves everyone time.

People who want speed only

If the goal is to do the current task faster and nothing else, a good prompt library will serve you better and cost less. That is a real answer, not a dismissal.

Organisations wanting a policy

We do not write AI usage policies. We help design workflows with controls in them, which is a different artefact and usually the thing the policy was trying and failing to achieve.

Anyone wanting output produced for them

If you want the book written, the report produced or the framework built on your behalf, this is the wrong company. Our entire premise is that you retain the capability.
