Future

The architecture is modular. Tell us which profession to build next.

The diagnostic core, the design method, the ARCHITECT standard and the evidence system already exist and are role-agnostic. Adding a profession means building its role overlay, reference workflow and domain standards: real work we do in the order demand justifies, weighing how many people ask and how specifically, how clear the profession's quality standards are, and whether client evidence can realistically be gathered there.

What already applies to you

If your profession has no pathway yet, the common core of the benchmark still applies. Task-selection judgment, human–AI allocation, verification practice, workflow integration, accountability, evidence practice and transfer into work are not role-specific constructs: they are what AI-augmented professional practice consists of, everywhere.

What you would not get yet is the role overlay: the profession-specific constructs, the reference workflow built to your domain's standards, and the escalation triggers that reflect your professional obligations rather than a generic set.

Signalling demand genuinely moves this. It is not a mailing-list capture with a roadmap attached.
