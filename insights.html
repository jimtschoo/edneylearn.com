Content pillars

What we will write about, and what we will not.

Pillar 01

The distinction between tool access and a redesigned production system, and why the gap between them is where most organisational AI investment quietly disappears.

Pillar 02

The workflow is the asset

Why the durable value is not the generated output but the reusable system that produces future outputs, and what that means for how you spend your time.

Pillar 03

Professional quality must be engineered

Explicit standards, verification, judgment and review gates. How to turn "I would know it if I saw it" into a check that runs every time.

Pillar 04

Professionals should retain intellectual control

Extending capability without erasing voice, originality, provenance or responsibility, and how to tell when a framework has stopped being yours.

Pillar 05

Standardised method, customised system

Why the design method should be common and the resulting workflow should not, and what goes wrong when organisations get this backwards.

Not a pillar

Generic AI news

Model release commentary, tool round-ups, prompt tips and takes on whatever was announced this week. There is enough of that, and none of it compounds.

Formats planned
